# Audit Knowledge Base - Audit Guidelines

## 1. Internal Audit Standards

### 1.1 Audit Planning
- All audits must have a documented audit plan approved by management
- Risk assessment must be performed before each audit cycle
- Audit scope should be clearly defined and communicated to all stakeholders
- Resource allocation should be based on risk priorities

### 1.2 Audit Execution
- Auditors must maintain independence and objectivity throughout the audit
- All audit evidence must be documented and properly referenced
- Sampling methodology should be statistically valid and documented
- Findings must be supported by sufficient and appropriate evidence

### 1.3 Audit Reporting
- Audit reports must be issued within 30 days of fieldwork completion
- All findings must be classified by severity (Critical, High, Medium, Low)
- Management responses must be obtained for all findings
- Root cause analysis should be included for significant findings

## 2. Compliance Requirements

### 2.1 Regulatory Compliance
- Annual compliance assessments must be conducted for all applicable regulations
- Regulatory changes must be tracked and impact assessments performed
- Compliance training must be provided to all relevant personnel
- Non-compliance incidents must be reported within 24 hours

### 2.2 Policy Compliance
- All corporate policies must be reviewed annually
- Policy exceptions must be documented and approved
- Policy violations must be escalated according to severity
- Policy awareness training is mandatory for all employees

## 3. Documentation Standards

### 3.1 Workpaper Requirements
- All workpapers must be prepared using approved templates
- Cross-referencing must be used to link evidence to findings
- Review notes must be cleared before report issuance
- Workpapers must be retained for a minimum of 7 years

### 3.2 Evidence Collection
- Original documents should be obtained when possible
- Electronic evidence must be properly authenticated
- Interview notes must be signed by the interviewee
- Photographic evidence should include date/time stamps

## 4. Risk Assessment Framework

### 4.1 Risk Identification
- Risk identification should cover strategic, operational, financial, and compliance risks
- Emerging risks must be monitored and assessed quarterly
- Risk owners must be assigned for each identified risk
- Risk registers must be maintained and updated regularly

### 4.2 Risk Evaluation
- Risks must be evaluated for likelihood and impact
- Risk appetite and tolerance levels must be defined
- Control effectiveness should be assessed for key risks
- Residual risk must be within acceptable tolerance levels

## 5. Follow-up Procedures

### 5.1 Remediation Tracking
- All audit findings must be tracked until closure
- Due dates must be established for each corrective action
- Extensions require management approval
- Overdue items must be escalated monthly

### 5.2 Verification Testing
- Remediation must be verified through testing
- Closure requires evidence of sustainable correction
- Recurring issues must trigger root cause analysis
- Systemic issues require process redesign
