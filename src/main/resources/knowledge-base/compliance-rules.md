# Compliance Rules and Regulations

## 1. Data Protection and Privacy

### 1.1 Personal Data Handling
- Personal data must only be collected for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes
- Data minimization principle: collect only what is necessary
- Data retention periods must be defined and enforced
- Data subject rights must be respected and facilitated

### 1.2 Data Security Requirements
- Personal data must be encrypted at rest and in transit
- Access to personal data must follow the principle of least privilege
- Data breaches must be reported within 72 hours
- Regular data protection impact assessments are required for high-risk processing

### 1.3 Cross-Border Data Transfer
- International data transfers require appropriate safeguards
- Standard contractual clauses must be in place for transfers outside approved regions
- Transfer impact assessments must be documented
- Data localization requirements must be identified and complied with

## 2. Financial Compliance

### 2.1 Accounting Standards
- Financial statements must comply with applicable accounting standards (GAAP/IFRS)
- Revenue recognition must follow prescribed criteria
- Asset valuation must be performed using approved methodologies
- Related party transactions must be disclosed appropriately

### 2.2 Internal Controls
- Segregation of duties must be maintained for financial processes
- Authorization limits must be defined and enforced
- Reconciliations must be performed and reviewed monthly
- Journal entries require appropriate documentation and approval

### 2.3 Financial Reporting
- Financial reports must be accurate, complete, and timely
- Quarterly and annual certifications are required
- Material weaknesses must be disclosed
- Remediation plans must be developed for control deficiencies

## 3. Anti-Money Laundering (AML)

### 3.1 Customer Due Diligence
- Know Your Customer (KYC) procedures must be followed for all customers
- Enhanced due diligence is required for high-risk customers
- Customer information must be updated periodically
- Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs) require enhanced monitoring

### 3.2 Transaction Monitoring
- Suspicious transactions must be identified and reported
- Transaction monitoring systems must be in place
- Alert investigation must be completed within defined timeframes
- Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs) must be filed when required

### 3.3 Sanctions Compliance
- Sanctions screening must be performed for all customers and transactions
- Positive matches must be investigated and resolved
- Blocked transactions must be reported to authorities
- Sanctions lists must be updated regularly

## 4. Employment Law Compliance

### 4.1 Hiring Practices
- Non-discriminatory hiring practices must be followed
- Background checks must comply with applicable laws
- Employment eligibility verification is required
- Equal opportunity policies must be in place

### 4.2 Workplace Safety
- Occupational health and safety regulations must be followed
- Workplace hazards must be identified and mitigated
- Safety training must be provided to all employees
- Incident reporting and investigation procedures must be in place

### 4.3 Employee Rights
- Minimum wage and overtime laws must be followed
- Leave entitlements must comply with applicable laws
- Anti-harassment policies must be implemented and enforced
- Whistleblower protection must be provided

## 5. Environmental Compliance

### 5.1 Environmental Permits
- Required environmental permits must be obtained and maintained
- Permit conditions must be monitored and complied with
- Permit renewals must be submitted timely
- Permit violations must be reported and remediated

### 5.2 Waste Management
- Waste must be properly classified and disposed of
- Hazardous waste handling must follow prescribed procedures
- Waste minimization efforts must be documented
- Waste disposal records must be maintained

### 5.3 Emissions Reporting
- Emissions must be monitored and reported as required
- Emission limits must not be exceeded
- Emission reduction targets must be established
- Carbon footprint reporting may be required
